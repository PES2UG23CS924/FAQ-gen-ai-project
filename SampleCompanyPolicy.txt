ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. – Company Policy and FAQ Document

1. Employment and Working Hours
ABC Technologies follows a standard 5-day work week from Monday to Friday.
Employees are expected to work between 9:30 AM and 6:30 PM.
Flexible working hours may be permitted with prior manager approval.
Overtime compensation is applicable only for approved work beyond regular hours.

2. Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid casual leave per calendar year.
In addition, employees receive 8 days of sick leave annually.
Sick leave exceeding two consecutive days requires submission of a valid medical certificate.
Unused casual or sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal.

3. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to team requirements.
Permanent remote work is not guaranteed and requires management approval.
Employees working remotely are expected to be available during core working hours.
Failure to respond during working hours may result in revocation of remote work privileges.

4. Salary and Payroll
Salaries are credited on or before the 5th working day of every month.
Salary slips are made available through the employee self-service portal.
Any discrepancies in payroll must be reported to HR within 7 working days.
The company is not responsible for delays caused by incorrect bank details.

5. Refund and Cancellation Policy
Customers may request a refund within 7 days of purchase.
Refund requests must be submitted through the official support email.
Approved refunds are processed within 7 working days.
Refunds are credited back to the original mode of payment.
Services already consumed or partially used are not eligible for refunds.

6. Customer Support
Customer support services are available from Monday to Friday.
Support hours are from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST.
Support requests can be raised via email or the customer support portal.
Responses are typically provided within 24 to 48 business hours.

7. Data Privacy and Security
ABC Technologies collects personal data such as name, email address, and contact number.
Customer data is used solely for service delivery and support purposes.
The company does not sell or share customer data with third parties without consent.
Users may request deletion of their personal data by contacting customer support.
All data is stored securely in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

8. Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior at all times.
Harassment, discrimination, or misconduct will not be tolerated.
Violation of company policies may lead to disciplinary action, including termination.
Employees must adhere to confidentiality agreements even after resignation.

9. Termination and Notice Period
Employees are required to serve a notice period of 30 days before resignation.
The company reserves the right to terminate employment in case of policy violations.
Final settlements are processed within 15 working days from the last working day.
Company assets must be returned before final settlement.

10. Policy Updates
ABC Technologies reserves the right to modify company policies at any time.
Employees and customers will be notified of major changes via official communication channels.
Continued use of services implies acceptance of updated policies.
